Privacy Policy

YES/NO Pulse — Evidence in. Signal out. One answer.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how YES/NO Pulse handles information when you use the YES/NO mobile application and related services.

YES/NO provides AI-assisted, evidence-led informational and entertainment guidance. It is not intended to be the sole basis for major life, medical, legal, financial, safety, or emergency decisions.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

2. Information You Choose to Submit

When you use an evidence-based analysis feature, you may choose to submit:

  • Your question.
  • Text or context you enter.
  • Screenshots or images you explicitly select.

Only submit information and content that you are authorized to share. Do not submit another person's private information without permission.

3. How Evidence Is Processed

Evidence that you explicitly submit is transmitted to our server for processing. Our server may send the submitted question, context, and selected images to OpenAI, a third-party AI service provider, in order to extract and evaluate evidence signals and generate an analysis.

OpenAI processes this information subject to its applicable API data and privacy policies.

4. What We Store

Our server does not intentionally keep submitted screenshot files after processing is completed.

To support consistent results and avoid unnecessary repeat processing, we may cache:

  • The extracted analysis result.
  • A technical fingerprint associated with the submitted evidence.
  • Limited technical information required to operate the service and enforce usage limits.

Your device may also store local app history and settings. Local information remains on your device unless you choose to remove it or otherwise use a feature that transmits it.

5. Installation and Usage Information

YES/NO may use an anonymous installation identifier and limited operational information to provide features such as usage limits, legal acceptance records, service integrity, troubleshooting, and analytics.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate, secure, understand, and improve the service.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store. We may receive subscription or entitlement status needed to determine whether Pro features are available to you.

We do not receive your full payment card information from Apple.

7. Advertising

The current launch version of YES/NO does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

If advertising or additional advertising-related data processing is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the applicable App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the app, provide requested analysis, maintain security, process purchases, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

OpenAI is used as a third-party AI processing provider for evidence analysis.

9. Your Controls

Depending on the feature, YES/NO provides controls that may allow you to:

  • Delete local history stored in the app.
  • Delete eligible server-side cached analysis data.
  • Choose whether or not to submit evidence for analysis.

You may also contact us regarding a privacy request at:

support@yesnopulse.app

10. Age

The launch version is intended for users aged 13 or older, or the minimum age required by local law if higher. Users under 18 must have permission from a parent or guardian to use the AI-powered analysis features.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled by the service. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our features, processing practices, service providers, or legal requirements change.

If changes are material, we may provide additional notice within the app where appropriate.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy or the YES/NO service can be sent to:

support@yesnopulse.app
